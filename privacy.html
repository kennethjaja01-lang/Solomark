Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Solomark Nig Ltd handles information you submit through the enquiry form on this website.

What we collect

Your name, organisation (optional), email address, telephone number, the service you need, project location, estimated budget (optional) and project description.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations and discuss your project. We do not sell your information.

Storage and sharing

Enquiries are stored in a secured database hosted by Supabase and the site is hosted by Vercel. These providers process data on our behalf. We share information with others only where needed to deliver your request or where the law requires it.

Retention and your rights

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to handle your request. You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
